## About this Project

The **Cybersecurity Homelab and Training** project was born out of a need for a reproducible, isolated, and complex lab environment that can be run on a single workstation. By leveraging nested virtualization, it simulates enterprise-grade infrastructure, allowing students and professionals to practice everything from network engineering to advanced penetration testing and incident response.
