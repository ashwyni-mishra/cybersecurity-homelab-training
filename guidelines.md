# Guidelines and Ethical Agreement

Welcome to CyberHomelab. Before you proceed with this curriculum, it is mandatory to read and understand the following guidelines and ethical boundaries. This project is designed for educational and research purposes only.

---

## 1. Intent of the Lab
The primary intent of CyberHomelab is to provide a safe, isolated, and legal environment for security professionals and students to:
- Learn the mechanics of virtualization and network engineering.
- Practice offensive security techniques against authorized targets.
- Develop and test defensive strategies and monitoring solutions.
- Understand the lifecycle of security incidents.

## 2. Ethical Use and Professional Conduct
As a user of this curriculum, you are expected to adhere to the highest standards of professional and ethical conduct:
- **Authorization**: Never use the techniques taught here against any system, network, or data for which you do not have explicit, written permission.
- **Isolation**: Always ensure your lab environment is properly isolated (using the "Dirty Pipe" configuration) to prevent accidental leakage of scanning or exploitation traffic.
- **Responsibility**: You are solely responsible for your actions and any consequences arising from the use of the tools and techniques described in this curriculum.

## 3. Legal Notice
The author, Ashwani Mishra, provides this curriculum as-is for educational purposes. Use of offensive tools in unauthorized environments may be a violation of local and international laws. By proceeding beyond this page, you acknowledge that:
- You will not use these skills for illegal or malicious purposes.
- You understand the legal risks associated with unauthorized hacking.
- You waive any liability against the author for actions taken by you or third parties using these materials.

---

## 4. Agreement to Proceed
**By using this curriculum, accessing the lessons, or deploying the lab infrastructure, you signify your agreement to these guidelines and the ethical boundaries outlined above.**

If you do not agree to these terms, you must immediately cease use of this material and remove any associated lab environments from your hardware.
