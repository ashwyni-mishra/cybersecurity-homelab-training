# 04-01: Offensive Methodology Overview

## Overview
Professional security testing follows structured methodologies to ensure thoroughness and repeatability. Two primary frameworks are the Cyber Kill Chain and PTES.

## Cyber Kill Chain (Lockheed Martin)
1. Reconnaissance
2. Weaponization
3. Delivery
4. Exploitation
5. Installation
6. Command and Control (C2)
7. Actions on Objectives
